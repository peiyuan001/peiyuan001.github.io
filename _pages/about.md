I am **Peiyuan Si**, and I received my Ph.D. degree from the College of Computing and Data Science, Nanyang Technological University (NTU), Singapore. My research focuses on secure and intelligent communication systems, with particular interests in post-quantum cryptography and semantic communication.

## Research Interests

* Post-Quantum Cryptography
* Semantic Communication
* Reinforcement Learning
* UAV Communication
* Intelligent and Secure Wireless Communications

## News

* **[2026]** Our paper *“Parameter Training Efficiency Aware Resource Allocation for AIGC in Space-Air-Ground Integrated Networks”* was published in **IEEE Transactions on Mobile Computing (TMC)**.
* **[2025]** Our paper *“Post-Deployment Fine-Tunable Semantic Communication”* was published in **IEEE Transactions on Wireless Communications (TWC)**.
