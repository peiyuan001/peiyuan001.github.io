Abstract:
Semantic communication (SemCom) is a novel approach aimed at boosting the efficacy of data exchange by leveraging the semantic essence of the content, which typically relies on a knowledge base (KB) and is often tailored for specific tasks or datasets. To enhance the adaptability of SemCom systems to unknown datasets, we introduce a post-deployment Fine-Tunable Semantic Communication (FTSC) system for image transmission, utilizing a two-tiered hierarchical vector quantized-variational autoencoder (VQ-VAE-2). Unlike conventional SemCom that operates on a limited range of pre-trained datasets, FTSC adjusts to varying input data after deployment, increasing its utility in various communication environments. FTSC integrates two innovative fine-tuning techniques: Decoder Fine-Tuning (DFT) and Latent Space-based Decoder Fine-Tuning (LSDFT). DFT updates the decoder with new images post-deployment without the need for gradient transmission, whereas LSDFT avoids transmitting raw images during fine-tuning. Extensive experiments affirm the performance improvement of FTSC, including compression rate and recovery quality, over Joint Photographic Experts Group (JPEG) and Joint Source-Channel Coding (JSCC) across multiple datasets and channel noise conditions.
